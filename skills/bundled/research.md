Conduct thorough research on the given topic:
1. Start with broad searches to understand the landscape
2. Follow up with specific queries on key subtopics
3. Fetch and analyze the most relevant pages
4. Cross-reference information from multiple sources
5. Synthesize findings into a well-organized report with:
   - Executive summary
   - Key findings with sources
   - Different perspectives or controversies
   - Conclusion and further reading
